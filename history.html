January 26, 1930 :

Indian National Congress declares Purna Swaraj (complete independence) from British rule.

August 15, 1947 :

India attains independence from British colonial rule.

August 29, 1947 :

A Drafting Committee is appointed with Dr. B.R. Ambedkar as its chairman to draft the Indian Constitution.

November 4, 1947 :

The Draft Constitution is prepared and submitted to the Constituent Assembly.

November 26, 1949 :

The Indian Constitution is adopted by the Constituent Assembly.

January 24, 1950 :

The Constituent Assembly members sign two handwritten copies of the Constitution, one in Hindi and one in English.

January 26, 1950 :

The Indian Constitution comes into effect, making India a republic. Dr. Rajendra Prasad becomes the first President of India.

January 26, 1950 :

The first Republic Day parade is held at Rajpath, New Delhi.

Every January 26 from 1950 onwards :

Republic Day is celebrated annually with grandeur, featuring parades showcasing India's cultural diversity and military strength.
