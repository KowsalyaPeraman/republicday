BHARAT RATHNA AWARD

One of the most prestigious awards presented on Republic Day is the Bharat Ratna. It is the highest civilian award in India and is given for exceptional service towards the nation. Recipients of the Bharat Ratna have made significant contributions in various fields such as public affairs, arts, literature, science, and social work.

The Bharat Ratna is the highest civilian award in India, instituted in 1954. It is awarded in recognition of "exceptional service/performance of the highest order," without distinction of race, occupation, position, or gender1. The award is presented by the President of India and recipients receive a Sanad (certificate) and a peepal leaf-shaped medallion.

PADMA AWARD

These are among the highest civilian honors in India, including the Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, and Padma Shri. They recognize distinguished service in various fields such as arts, literature, science, public affairs, and social service.

  1. Padma Vibhushan: Awarded for exceptional and distinguished service in any field, including service rendered by government servants.
  2. Padma Bhushan: Awarded for distinguished service of a high order in any field.
  3. Padma Shri: Awarded for distinguished service in any field.

GALLANTRY AWARD

These include the Param Vir Chakra, Ashoka Chakra, and Kirti Chakra, awarded for acts of bravery and valor in the face of the enemy

About Gallantry Awards Gallantry Awards in India honor acts of bravery and self-sacrifice by individuals in various fields, especially in the military and police forces. These awards recognize exceptional courage in the face of adversity.


  • Nominations:Received from armed forces, police, and other government bodies.
  • Committee Review:Reviewed by a committee of distinguished individuals.
  • President's Approval:Final approval by the President of India.
  • Ceremony:Presented during Republic Day and Independence Day ceremonies.

NATIONAL BRAVERY AWARD

NATIONAL BRAVERY AWARD The National Bravery Award is a prestigious honor given annually to children below the age of 18 for acts of exceptional bravery against all odds. Instituted in 1957, the award is organized by the Indian Council for Child Welfare (ICCW) and presented by the Prime Minister of India on the eve of Republic Day1.

These are among the highest civilian honors in India, including the Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, and Padma Shri. They recognize distinguished service in various fields such as arts, literature, science, public affairs, and social service.

  • Selection process:Nominations are invited from official agencies such as local and district governments, school authorities, and councils for child welfare. A high-level committee reviews the applications and selects the awardees
  • Award ceremony:The awards are usually announced on Children's Day (November 14) or in January the following year, and presented by the Prime Minister on Republic Day.
  • Notable recipients:Over the years, many children have been honored with this award for their courageous acts, including saving lives, confronting kidnappers, and showing bravery in various challenging situations.
